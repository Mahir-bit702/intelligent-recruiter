# 🎬 Video Demo Script — Intelligent Recruiter
# Target: 3–4 minutes | Screen record your screen + voiceover

---

## [0:00–0:20] INTRO (Slide 1 on screen)

"Hi, we're Team [Name] from APU, and this is the Intelligent Recruiter —
an AI agent that replaces static job boards with a 4-step reasoning pipeline
that finds the best candidates, and checks its own results for bias."

---

## [0:20–0:45] THE PROBLEM (Slide 2 on screen)

"Traditional recruiting tools match keywords — not potential.
75% of qualified candidates are rejected before a human ever reads their resume.
And 39% of hiring decisions carry unconscious bias.

Our agent solves both problems."

---

## [0:45–1:15] SHOW THE ARCHITECTURE (Slide 4 on screen)

"Here's how it works — 4 specialized agents in sequence:

First, the JD Parser reads any job description and extracts structured requirements.
Then the Candidate Analyzer scores each person across 4 dimensions.
The Bias Checker — our unique feature — audits those scores for fairness.
Finally the Ranking Agent produces a final report for the hiring manager."

---

## [1:15–2:45] LIVE DEMO (Switch to the prototype)

[Open IntelligentRecruiter in browser]

"Let me show you the live system."

[Point to the Input tab]
"We have a job description for a Senior Full-Stack Developer at a fintech company,
and 4 candidates. I'll hit Launch Pipeline now."

[Click the button — watch agents run]
"You can see each agent completing in sequence — JD Parser done,
Candidate Analyzer done, Bias Checker running, Ranking complete."

[Switch to Results tab]
"Here are our results. Alice Tan is our top pick with a score of 87 — STRONG YES.

Let me click on her card to expand it."

[Expand Alice's card]
"Score breakdown: 90% must-have match, strong experience, high domain relevance.
Her strengths are FastAPI experience, AWS certification, and payment system background.
One gap — no formal ML experience."

[Scroll to bias section]
"And importantly — our Bias Checker flagged a LOW risk with a fairness score of 91.
No significant bias was detected in this evaluation.

This is something no other recruitment tool does automatically."

---

## [2:45–3:20] IMPACT & SCALABILITY (Slide 8 on screen)

"This system can screen 100 candidates in under 2 minutes.
It's fully deployable — no backend server needed.
And the agent architecture means we can add new agents —
like an interview question generator, or ATS integration — without rebuilding anything.

The future roadmap includes PDF resume upload, HR dashboards,
and a public API for job platforms."

---

## [3:20–3:45] CLOSE (Slide 10 on screen)

"We're a team of 4 from APU — built this during AI Marathon 2026.

The Intelligent Recruiter shows that LLMs aren't just chatbots —
they can be orchestrated agents that solve real hiring problems,
fairly and at scale.

Thank you."

---

## RECORDING TIPS:
- Use QuickTime (Mac) or Xbox Game Bar (Windows) to screen record
- Speak slowly and clearly — don't rush
- Keep cursor movements deliberate
- Do a test run first to check timing
- Edit out any mistakes with CapCut or iMovie
- Add background music at 20% volume (lo-fi works well)
- Export as MP4, max 500MB
